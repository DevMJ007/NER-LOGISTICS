ABOUT

What is NER Logistics Intelligence?

An AI-powered platform that combines weather data, flood monitoring, satellite observations, field reports and GPS tracking to provide real-time disaster intelligence for the North Eastern Region of India.

Weather Monitoring

Real-time temperature, rainfall, wind speed and humidity from Open-Meteo API.

Flood Detection

Hydrological river discharge monitoring across major rivers and tributaries.

Satellite Intel

Copernicus Sentinel-1 radar and Sentinel-2 multispectral environmental analysis.

AI Risk Engine

Machine learning analyzes all data sources to predict disaster risk levels.
