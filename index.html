The Platform

Multi-Source Intelligence
for a Vulnerable Region

Northeast India faces floods, landslides, earthquakes and infrastructure disruptions daily. NER Intelligence brings every data source together — weather sensors, satellite imagery, river monitoring and field reports — into a single, actionable view.

01

Weather Intelligence

Real-time temperature, rainfall, wind and humidity from Open-Meteo. Hourly forecasts and severe weather alerts for all eight states.

02

Flood Detection

Hydrological river discharge monitoring across Brahmaputra and tributaries. Early warning for rising water levels and flood risk.

03

Satellite Intel

Copernicus Sentinel radar and multispectral imagery for vegetation, land cover and environmental change detection.

04

AI Risk Engine

Machine learning correlates all data sources to classify risk levels and predict disruption probability across corridors.

Features

From Raw Data to
Actionable Decisions

A complete intelligence pipeline — collect, analyze, visualize and respond. Every feature designed for operational clarity in high-stakes environments.

🗺️

Interactive GIS Mapping

Leaflet-powered real-time visualization with layer controls for weather, flood, satellite and risk overlays across the region.

📡

Real-Time Streams

WebSocket connections deliver live sensor readings, weather updates and incident reports as they happen.

⚠️

Smart Alerts

Threshold-based and AI-driven alerts for extreme weather, flooding, road closures and accessibility changes.

📊

Risk Dashboard

Centralized KPI grid showing risk levels, weather summaries, active incidents and connectivity status at a glance.

🏔️

Route Analysis

OSRM-powered accessibility analysis showing road connectivity, blocked routes and alternate paths across corridors.

📝

Field Reports

Ground-truth data collection from field teams with geotagged incident reports and severity classification.

Process

Four Stages of
Intelligence

From raw sensor data to real-time response — a systematic pipeline that transforms information into life-saving decisions.

01

Collect

Gather data from weather APIs, flood sensors, satellite imagery, GIS databases and field reports across all eight states.

02

Analyze

Spatial queries, AI risk modeling and multi-source correlation identify patterns, threats and anomalies in real time.

03

Understand

Generate risk scores, accessibility maps and disruption reports for each region and transport corridor.

04

Respond

Deliver alerts, action briefs and decision support to field teams and command centers in real time.
